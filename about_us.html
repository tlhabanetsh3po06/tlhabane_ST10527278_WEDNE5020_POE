Pretoria Auto Spares is a family-owned private company based in Pretoria, Gauteng. The business started in 2018 as a small workshop supplying spare parts to local taxi drivers and has grown to serve mechanics, fleet owners and private vehicle owners across Gauteng.

At Pretoria Auto Spares we provide, genuine auto parts with fast and reliable sevice to all motorists in Pretoria.

We strive to be the most trusted auto spares supplier in Gauteng by 2030!

Our target audience is the general customer, "people who own vehicles and use them daily to ensure that their vehicles are safe to drive" and not particularly focused on the upper class customer but it is a market we would like to break into.
