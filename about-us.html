About Pretoria Auto Spares Hub

Genuine Parts. Fast Service. Pretoria Driven.

Our Story

Pretoria Auto Spares is a family-owned private company based in Pretoria, Gauteng. The business started in 2018 as a small workshop supplying spare parts to local taxi drivers and has grown to serve mechanics, fleet owners and private vehicle owners across Gauteng.

At Pretoria Auto Spares we provide genuine auto parts with fast and reliable service to all motorists in Pretoria.

We strive to be the most trusted auto spares supplier in Gauteng by 2030.

Who We Serve

Our target audience is the general customer: people who own vehicles and use them daily and need them to be safe to drive. We are not focused only on the upper class customer, but it is a market we would like to break into.

  • Mechanics
  • Taxi associations
  • Uber and Bolt drivers
  • Private car owners aged 20 to 55
